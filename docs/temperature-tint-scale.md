# Temperature Tint Scale

Recommended 9-tint temperature scale aligned with the **26 °C alarm default**.

| # | Color | Temperature threshold |
|---:|---|---:|
| 1 | Blue — deep (`#2563EB`) | ≤ 10 °C |
| 2 | Blue — medium (`#3B82F6`) | 10–15 °C |
| 3 | Blue — light (`#93C5FD`) | 15–18 °C |
| 4 | Green — deep (`#166534`) | 18–20 °C |
| 5 | Green — medium (`#16A34A`) | 20–22 °C |
| 6 | Green — light (`#86EFAC`) | 22–24 °C |
| 7 | Red — light (`#FCA5A5`) | 24–25 °C |
| 8 | Red — medium (`#EF4444`) | 25–26 °C |
| 9 | Red — deep (`#991B1B`) | ≥ 26 °C |

## Alarm alignment

The red range begins at 24 °C to signal that the temperature is approaching the default **26 °C alarm threshold**. The darkest red applies at and above 26 °C.

*Note: These are proposed tint thresholds, not verified device-specific settings.*
