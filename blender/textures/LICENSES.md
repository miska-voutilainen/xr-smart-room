## Made for this project

- Generated by our scripts (PIL, no outside imagery): `s1_entrance/blind_slats.png`, `s1_lab/screen_*.png`,
  `s1_lab/keys.png`, `s2_board/qr_atlas.png`, `s2_tableware/cup_*_wrap.png` (drawn over the CC0 paper scans).
- Crops of our own photos of the lab (TalTech Virumaa College), perspective-rectified, metadata stripped:
  `s1_lab/board_*.jpg` (demo boards; people in the original photos were cloned out), `s1_lab/inst_*.jpg`,
  `s2_board/device_fronts.png`, `s1_entrance/poster_*_120945.jpg`, all of `s3_hall/`.
  Some of these show public signage in the building: the TalTech "Good practice for learning and teaching" posters
  (`s3_hall/poster_pg.jpg`, `s1_entrance/poster_pink_120945.jpg`, `s3_hall/poster_a3.jpg`) and the staff cut-out
  board on the teal wall (`s3_hall/cutout.png`) contain photos of real people printed on the signage.
  The poster artwork belongs to TalTech; replace these before any use outside this student project.
