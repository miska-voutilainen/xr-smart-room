# 08 Nest Speaker

Real device: Google Nest / Sonos speaker (speaker1)

## vr_click.mp4: click in VR, result in VR
- What is clicked: Point at the top of the speaker and press the trigger.
- What happens in VR: The speaker starts playing (light ring on).

## real_click.mp4: click in the real lab, result in VR
- What to do in real life: Start music on the real speaker and turn the volume up.
- What the bridge sends and what happens in VR: speaker1.state playing, volume 50 > 80. The virtual speaker shows playback and the volume level.
